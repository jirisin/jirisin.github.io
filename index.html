How I use

AI in my work

All of this is still experimental. I'm actively finding out what works and what doesn't — and happy to adopt whatever way of working makes the work faster or better.

01 · UX design & research

A second brain for UX research.

For my latest project I built a dedicated repository — interview transcripts, reports, product vision, every bit of context I could capture — as structured markdown files. I use it to discuss, plan and prioritise my UX work, and to draft some of the UX deliverables themselves.

02 · UI design

Inspiration, not production — with one exception.

For high-fidelity screens I'm still faster in Figma than prompting one into existence — AI hasn't yet produced UI good enough that redoing it beats designing it from scratch.

The exception: adopting a brand-new design system with no Figma counterpart. I used Figma MCP to generate the tokens and components, building a dedicated AI skill around my requirements. A few days of setup made me markedly faster than building it all by hand.

03 · Code

Shipping PRs, not just handoffs.

I can implement simpler features myself and open PRs developers review — often skipping Figma entirely. It's one of the fastest paths from idea to shipped change I have.

04 · Product ownership

Prioritising with the same second brain.

The same repository tracks problems and assumptions, prioritised and planned as now / next / later — so product decisions and UX work draw on one shared, evolving source of context.
